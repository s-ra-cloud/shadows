- before: Vast turtle/tortoise; back broad enough to support Mount Mandara.
- after:  Giant turtle or tortoise, broad back

**characterTrait**

- before: supporting, immovable, vast, cosmic-foundation, patient
- after:  supportive, immovable, vast, foundational, patient

## Hiranyakashipu

**domain**

- before: demon-king; father of the Viṣṇu-devotee Prahlāda; slain by Narasiṃha; the recipient of Brahmā's circumvented boon
- after:  demon kingship, tyranny, hostility to gods, self-deification, persecution of devotion, boon-granted invulnerability

**physicalCharacteristics**

- before: Mighty demon-king; immense, royal-attired; bearing weapons; tyrannical countenance.
- after:  immense stature, regal attire, armed with weapons, fierce expression

**characterTrait**

- before: proud, boon-blessed, anti-Viṣṇu, tyrannical, son-persecuting, self-deifying
- after:  proud, boon-blessed, hostile to gods, tyrannical, son-persecuting, self-deifying

## Mahishasura

**domain**

- before: the buffalo-demon — chief asura defeated all male gods, against whom Durgā was created and by whom slain in the central narrative of the Devī Māhātmya
- after:  war, conquest, tyranny, enemy of gods, buffalo demon

**physicalCharacteristics**

- before: In his demon-king form, mighty asura warrior; in his buffalo form, vast water-buffalo; can shapeshift between forms in battle.
- after:  demon king, massive water buffalo

**characterTrait**

- before: shapeshifter, boon-blessed, tyrannical, defeats-of-gods
- after:  shape-shifting, boon-blessed, tyrannical, god-defeating

## Yama (Hindu)

**domain**

- before: god of death; lord of the dead; first man to die; judge of the dead; lord of southern direction; son of Sūrya; twin of Yamī
- after:  death, lord of the dead, judge of the dead, southern direction

**physicalCharacteristics**

- before: Dark-complexioned, two- or four-armed; bears noose and mace; rides great water-buffalo; often with Citragupta beside him.
- after:  Dark-complexioned, two or four arms, holds noose and mace, rides a water buffalo

**characterTrait**

- before: just, terrible, impartial, inescapable, great teacher of mortality, kind-to-the-righteous, fearsome-to-the-wicked
- after:  just, terrible, impartial, inescapable, instructive, kind, fearsome
