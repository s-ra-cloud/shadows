The 1769 King James Bible, as revised by Benjamin Blayney.

198The King James Bible
​

THE
HOLY BIBLE,
CONTAINING THE
Old and New Testaments:
Translated out of
The Original Tongues:
AND WITH THE
FORMER TRANSLATIONS
Diligently Compared and Revised,
By His MAJESTY's Special Command.

Appointed to be read in Churches.

OXFORD,

Printed by T. Wright and W. Gill, Printers to the UNIVERSITY:
And sold by R. Baldwin, and S. Crowder, in Paternoster Row, London; and by W. Jackson, in Oxford. 1769.
